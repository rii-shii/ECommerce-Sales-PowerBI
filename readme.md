# Technova Solutions — Sales & Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Technova Solutions: Sales & Performance Dashboard** is a business intelligence project built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer segments, and payment preferences.

The dashboard brings key performance indicators (KPIs) and interactive visualizations together in one place, helping users explore business performance and identify important sales trends.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales, profit, order volume, and quantity.
- Compare profitability across regions.
- Identify top-performing products by sales.
- Explore the relationship between sales and profit.
- Understand sales distribution across customer segments and product categories.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Compare customer payment preferences.
- Enable interactive data exploration using dashboard slicers.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Microsoft Power BI** — dashboard development and data visualization
- **Data visualization** — KPI cards, bar charts, line charts, column charts, and donut charts
- **Interactive slicers** — filtering by product name, profit, and sales

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Profit by Region
A horizontal bar chart compares profitability across four regions.

- North: 26K
- South: 16K
- West: 14K
- East: 4K

**Insight:** North records the highest profit, while East records the lowest among the four regions.

### 2. Top 10 Products
A horizontal bar chart ranks products by sales value.

- Samsung Galaxy S24: 75K
- OnePlus 13: 65K
- Other products include office furniture, webcams, storage boxes, binders, and wireless mice.

**Insight:** The Samsung Galaxy S24 and OnePlus 13 are the leading products shown in the dashboard.

### 3. Profit by Sales
A line chart explores how profit changes as sales increase.

**Purpose:** Examine the relationship between sales value and profit and identify patterns that may warrant further investigation.

### 4. Sales by Segment
A donut chart shows how sales are distributed across customer segments.

- Consumer: 350K (67.95%)
- Two other segments: 79K and 86K

**Insight:** The Consumer segment contributes the largest share of sales.

### 5. Sales by Category
A column chart compares sales across product categories.

- Technology: 0.39M
- Furniture: 0.11M
- Office Supplies: 0.01M

**Insight:** Technology contributes the largest sales value among the categories displayed.

### 6. Sales by Order Date
A line chart displays daily sales trends from early January through February 8.

**Purpose:** Explore changes in daily sales, identify peaks and dips, and investigate periods of stronger or weaker performance.

### 7. Sales by Payment Mode
A donut chart compares sales across payment methods.

| Payment Mode | Sales | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| UPI | 199K | 38.63% |
| Credit Card | 157K | 30.57% |
| Debit Card | 148K | 28.67% |
| Cash | 11K | Remaining share |

**Insight:** UPI is the largest payment channel by sales value in the dashboard, followed by credit cards and debit cards.

## 🎛️ Interactive Features

The dashboard includes dropdown slicers for:

- Product Name
- Profit
- Sales

These slicers allow users to explore the displayed results using different filter selections.

## 💡 Key Business Takeaways

- **Regional performance:** North is the leading region by profit.
- **Product performance:** Samsung Galaxy S24 and OnePlus 13 lead the displayed product ranking.
- **Category performance:** Technology accounts for the largest share of sales.
- **Customer segments:** Consumer customers contribute approximately 68% of sales.
- **Payment preferences:** UPI represents the largest payment-mode share.
- **Sales monitoring:** Daily sales and profit-versus-sales visuals provide opportunities to investigate business trends.
